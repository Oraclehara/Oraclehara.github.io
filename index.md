# Hestia Mail Manager

Hestia Mail Manager is a personal email-management workflow
operated by Ken Yap using Hermes on a personal Mac mini.

It accesses only the Gmail account authorized by its owner.
It classifies messages, applies labels, archives eligible mail,
and saves selected tasks, purchase records, financial documents,
personal records and attachments to the owner's Notion workspace.

Email content may be processed by the AI provider configured
in Hermes. This is a personal workflow, not a public service.

Sending email, permanent deletion and automatic timed trashing
are disabled by the workflow's rules.
