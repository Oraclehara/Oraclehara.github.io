# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Hestia Mail Manager is operated by Ken Yap for personal use.

## Data accessed

With the account owner's Google authorization, the workflow
accesses Gmail message content, headers, labels, message and
thread identifiers, and attachments.

## Purpose

Data is used to classify and organize email, identify tasks,
and preserve selected purchases, financial documents and
personal records in the owner's Notion workspace.

## Processing and storage

Hermes runs on the owner's Mac mini. Email content may be
sent to the AI provider configured in Hermes for classification
and extraction. Selected records and attachments are uploaded
to Notion.

OAuth credentials, processing records and restoration information
are stored locally. Temporary attachment files may also be
stored locally during processing. Local backups may retain copies.

## Sharing

The workflow uses Google, the configured AI provider and Notion
to perform its functions. It does not sell Gmail data or use it
for advertising.

## Retention and removal

Records saved to Notion remain until the owner removes them.
Local records and backups remain until the owner removes them.
Revoking Google access stops future authorized Gmail access;
it does not automatically delete previously saved copies.

## Controls

The owner can revoke access through their Google Account
permissions and disable the scheduled Hermes jobs.

Sending email, permanent deletion and automatic timed trashing
are disabled by workflow rules. These rules are separate from
the broader permissions granted by Google.
